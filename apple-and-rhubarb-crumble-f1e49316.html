Apple and Rhubarb Crumble

Yield: 6 (as saved) · Prep: 20 min · Cook: 40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 medium apples , peeled, cored and sliced (approximately 600 g prepared weight)
  • 400 g rhubarb stalks, cut into 2-3 cm pieces
  • 70 g brown sugar, plus extra to taste, for fruit
  • 8 g cornflour
  • 5 ml vanilla extract
  • 1.5 g ground cinnamon
  • 30 ml water
  • 150 g plain flour
  • 70 g rolled oats
  • 110 g brown sugar, for crumble
  • 100 g cold butter, diced
  • 1 pinch salt
  • Butter, for greasing the baking dish

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C conventional or 160°C fan-forced. Lightly butter a roughly 2-litre baking dish.
  2. In a large bowl, toss the prepared apples and rhubarb with 70 g brown sugar, cornflour, vanilla extract, cinnamon and water. Spoon the mixture into the prepared baking dish. If the rhubarb is especially tart, add a little extra sugar to taste.
  3. In a separate bowl, combine the plain flour, rolled oats, 110 g brown sugar and salt. Add the cold diced butter and rub it into the dry ingredients with your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some small clumps.
  4. Scatter the crumble mixture evenly over the fruit. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the topping is golden, the fruit filling is bubbling and the apples are tender when pierced. Loosely cover with aluminium foil if the topping browns too quickly.
  5. Remove from the oven and allow to rest for 10 minutes. Serve warm with custard, cream or vanilla ice cream.
