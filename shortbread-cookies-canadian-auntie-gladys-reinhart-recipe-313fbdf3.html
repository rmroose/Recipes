Shortbread Cookies (Canadian Auntie Gladys Reinhart Recipe)

Ingredients

  • 454 g butter , at room temperature (Lurpak preferred)
  • 120 g pure icing sugar
  • 500 g plain flour
  • 5 ml vanilla extract

Method

  1. Bring the butter to room temperature, ideally 22°C. Cream the butter until light and fluffy.
  2. Sift in the icing sugar and add the vanilla extract. Mix until combined.
  3. Gradually sift in the flour. Knead the dough very thoroughly; the more it is kneaded, the better the texture.
  4. Preheat the oven to 177°C. Form the dough into shapes using a cookie press or by hand and arrange on baking trays.
  5. Bake at 177°C for 10–12 minutes, or longer for thicker shapes. Check after 8 minutes. The bottoms should be very light brown. Avoid dark colouring, which indicates overbaking.
  6. For cookie press consistency, maintain a room temperature of approximately 22°C. If the dough becomes too soft to pass through the cookie press properly, chill it in the freezer for approximately 5 minutes, adjusting according to room temperature.
