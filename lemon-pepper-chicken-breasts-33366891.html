Lemon Pepper Chicken Breasts

Yield: 4 (as saved) · Prep: 12 min · Cook: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 700–800 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 2 large breasts)
  • 18 g lemon pepper seasoning, divided
  • 1.6 g kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 95 g all-purpose flour
  • 22 ml vegetable oil
  • 55 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
  • 120 ml chicken stock
  • 80 ml freshly squeezed lemon juice (from about 2 lemons)
  • 15 g Italian parsley, chopped
  • Lemon slices, for garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Place the palm of your hand flat against the top of a chicken breast to prevent it from sliding. Holding your knife horizontally to the cutting board, slice through the breast to create 2 thinner cutlets. Repeat with the remaining chicken breast.
  2. In a small bowl, combine 16 g lemon pepper seasoning with 1.6 g kosher salt. Place the flour in a separate shallow dish. Season both sides of each chicken cutlet with the spice mixture. Dredge the seasoned chicken in the flour, shaking off any excess. Transfer to a wire rack or platter while heating the pan.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil and 30 g butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Once the butter melts, swirl the pan to combine the fats. When the oil begins to shimmer, arrange the chicken cutlets in a single layer, cooking in batches if necessary. Fry undisturbed for 3 minutes. Flip with tongs and cook for another 2½–3 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and reaches an internal temperature of 74°C. Transfer the cooked chicken to a platter.
  4. Discard the darkened fat from the skillet. Return the skillet to medium heat and melt the remaining 25 g butter. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 30–45 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the chicken stock and lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Bring the liquid to a boil and cook for approximately 1 minute, until slightly thickened. Season the sauce with the remaining 2 g lemon pepper seasoning and additional salt to taste.
  5. Return the chicken cutlets to the skillet once the sauce has reduced slightly. Turn the chicken to coat both sides evenly in the lemon pepper sauce, then turn off the heat. Allow the residual heat to warm the chicken through. Sprinkle with chopped Italian parsley, garnish with optional lemon slices, and serve immediately with rice or mashed potatoes.
