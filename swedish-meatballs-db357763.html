Swedish Meatballs

Yield: 6 (as saved) · Prep: 20 min · Cook: 60 min

Ingredients

  • 90 g bread, crusts removed and cubed (approximately 3 slices)
  • 60 ml milk
  • 14 g butter, for cooking onion
  • 100 g yellow or white onion, peeled and grated
  • 1 large egg
  • 340 g ground beef
  • 227 g ground pork
  • 5 g kosher salt
  • 2 g ground black pepper
  • 1 g freshly ground nutmeg
  • 1 g ground cardamom
  • 42 g butter, for browning meatballs
  • 42 g butter, for gravy
  • 24 g plain flour
  • 480 ml beef stock
  • 60 g full-fat sour cream
  • 40 g lingonberry, red currant, raspberry or cranberry jelly (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Place the cubed bread in a large bowl and mix with the milk. Let sit for 5 to 10 minutes until the bread has absorbed all the milk. Shred the softened bread using a fork or your hands.
  2. Melt 14 g butter in a sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the grated onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until translucent and softened. Remove from the heat and allow to cool briefly.
  3. Add the cooled onion to the soaked bread mixture. Add the egg, ground beef, ground pork, kosher salt, black pepper, nutmeg and cardamom. Mix thoroughly with clean hands until evenly combined. If time permits, refrigerate the mixture for approximately 20 minutes to make shaping easier.
  4. Shape the mixture into approximately 40 meatballs, each 3 to 4 cm in diameter. Arrange on a plate or baking tray. The mixture will be slightly wet, so handle gently.
  5. Heat 42 g butter in a large sauté pan over medium heat until melted and foamy. Working in batches to avoid overcrowding, add the meatballs and brown slowly on all sides. Turn carefully using tongs or a wooden spoon to prevent them breaking apart. Cook until just cooked through, then transfer to a bowl. Wipe the pan clean with paper towel to remove used butter and browned or burnt residue.
  6. Add 42 g fresh butter to the cleaned pan over medium heat. Once melted and bubbling, gradually whisk in 24 g plain flour until smooth. Continue stirring and cooking for several minutes until the roux turns light brown, approximately the colour of coffee with cream.
  7. Gradually pour 480 ml beef stock into the roux while whisking continuously. The mixture may initially sputter or thicken considerably. Continue adding stock slowly and stirring until the gravy becomes smooth, silky and evenly thickened.
  8. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in 60 g full-fat sour cream. If using jelly, stir in approximately 40 g now or reserve it for serving. Taste the gravy and adjust with salt and black pepper as required, starting with approximately 2.5 g kosher salt and 0.5 g black pepper.
  9. Return the browned meatballs to the pan with the gravy. Cook gently over low heat for several minutes until heated through and fully cooked. Avoid boiling the sauce vigorously, as the sour cream may curdle.
  10. Serve the Swedish meatballs and creamy gravy over mashed potatoes or egg noodles. Alternatively, serve individual meatballs with gravy as an appetiser. Offer additional lingonberry or other berry jelly on the side if desired.
