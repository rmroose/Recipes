Lemon Yoghurt Bowl

1 serving · 5 minutes · No cooking

An original demonstration recipe for checking recipe recognition in Safari on HomeView.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain yoghurt
  • 80 g blueberries
  • 20 g rolled oats
  • 5 ml lemon juice

Method

  1. Spoon the yoghurt into a serving bowl.
  2. Wash the blueberries and add them to the yoghurt.
  3. Sprinkle over the oats, add the lemon juice and serve.
