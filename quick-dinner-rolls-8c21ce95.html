Quick Dinner Rolls

Yield: 15 (as saved) · Prep: 40 min · Cook: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 180 ml milk (whole, 2% or 1%)
  • 180 ml water
  • 25 g sugar
  • 12 g active-dry or instant yeast
  • 435–500 g all-purpose flour, plus extra if needed
  • 6 g salt
  • 30 g unsalted or salted butter, melted, plus extra for greasing the pan

Method

  1. Combine the milk and water in a measuring cup and microwave for approximately 30 seconds, until lukewarm. Pour into the bowl of a stand mixer and stir in the sugar and yeast. Set aside for 1 minute until the yeast dissolves and small bubbles appear. If the yeast may be expired, allow 3–5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Stir 435 g flour and the salt into the liquid using a stiff spatula until a shaggy dough forms. Knead with a dough hook on low speed for 5 minutes, or by hand for 10 minutes, until smooth. If the dough sticks excessively after 30 seconds, add approximately 15 g flour and knead for another 30 seconds. Repeat if necessary, avoiding too much flour. The dough should remain slightly sticky.
  3. Gather the dough into a ball and return it to the bowl. Cover with plastic wrap or a plate and leave to rise for 10 minutes. This resting period can be skipped if short on time.
  4. Position an oven rack in the lower-middle position and preheat the oven to 200°C. Lightly grease a 23 × 33 cm baking dish or cake pan with butter or cooking spray.
  5. Turn the dough onto a clean work surface. Divide into 15 equal portions. Shape each portion into a smooth, taut ball by cupping your palm over the dough and rolling it in tight circles against the counter. Dust lightly with flour only if necessary.
  6. Arrange the shaped rolls in the prepared pan in 3 rows of 5, leaving a little space between them. Cover and allow to rise for 15 minutes. The rolls should become slightly puffy, although they may not rise noticeably.
  7. Brush the tops with melted butter. Bake for 15–18 minutes, rotating the pan halfway through for even baking. The rolls are ready when puffed and deep golden-brown on top.
  8. Allow the rolls to cool briefly in the pan. Lift them out with a spatula and transfer to a wire rack until cool enough to serve. Store leftovers in an airtight container for 2–3 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
