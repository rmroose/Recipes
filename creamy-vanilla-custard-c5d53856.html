Creamy Vanilla Custard

Yield: 6 (as saved)

Ingredients

  • 500 ml full-cream milk
  • 150 ml thickened cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 38 g caster sugar
  • 5 g cornflour
  • 5 ml vanilla extract or paste

Method

  1. Warm the milk and cream in a saucepan over medium-low heat until steaming, but do not boil.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks, caster sugar and cornflour in a bowl until smooth. Slowly whisk in the warm milk mixture.
  3. Return the mixture to the saucepan. Stir constantly over low heat for about 5–8 minutes, until the custard lightly coats the back of a spoon. Do not allow it to boil, as the eggs may scramble.
  4. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract or paste. Strain for an extra-smooth consistency if desired, then serve warm over crumble.
