Crispy Sweet Chilli Chicken

Yield: 6 (as saved) · Prep: 15 min · Cook: 40 min

Ingredients

  • 35 g cornflour
  • 5.5 g sweet smoked paprika
  • 7 g garlic granules
  • 2 eggs
  • 9 ml soy sauce
  • 1 kg boneless, skinless chicken thighs, chopped into bite-sized pieces
  • Vegetable oil for deep-frying, enough for 3 cm depth in the pan
  • 18 ml sesame oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, finely grated
  • 35 ml dark soy sauce
  • 35 ml rice wine
  • 55 ml rice vinegar
  • 250 ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 35–55 ml honey, to taste
  • 3.2 g sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Method

  1. Combine the cornflour, paprika, garlic granules, eggs and soy sauce in a large bowl. Beat until smooth. Add the chicken pieces and mix thoroughly to coat them evenly in the batter.
  2. Pour vegetable oil into a large, deep pan to a depth of approximately 3 cm, ensuring the pan is no more than one-third full. Heat over medium heat until a small pinch of batter sizzles immediately. Add the chicken in a single layer, working in batches if necessary. Fry for 3–4 minutes on each side until crisp, golden and cooked through. Check the internal temperature with a digital thermometer; the original recipe specifies 70°C, but chicken should reach at least 74°C for food safety. Transfer to a plate lined with kitchen paper to drain. Repeat with the remaining chicken.
  3. To prepare the sauce, heat the sesame oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the grated garlic and ginger and cook for a few minutes until softened and fragrant, taking care not to let them brown.
  4. Stir in the dark soy sauce, rice wine, rice vinegar and sweet chilli sauce. Bring to a gentle simmer. Add 18 ml honey initially and taste. Gradually add more honey, up to 55 ml total, if additional sweetness is needed. The finished sauce should balance sweetness, spice and acidity.
  5. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce and stir gently until every piece is completely coated. Serve immediately, scattered with sesame seeds and finely sliced spring onions, with cooked rice on the side.
